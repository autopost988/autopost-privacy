Privacy Policy for Auto Post

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Auto Post handles information when authorized users use the application to manage and publish content to Facebook Pages through the services and permissions made available by Meta.

1. Information We Access

Depending on the features used and the permissions granted, Auto Post may access:

  • Facebook Page identifiers and Page information needed to identify Pages the user is authorized to manage.
  • Telegram user IDs and chat IDs needed for communication with the bot.
  • Text, captions, media links, and scheduling information supplied by the user for publishing.
  • Technical logs, such as timestamps and error messages, when generated by the application.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the requested features, including selecting authorized Pages, preparing content, scheduling posts, publishing content, sending bot messages, and troubleshooting technical problems.

3. Facebook and Meta Platform Data

Information obtained through Meta services is used only for the features requested by the authorized user and in accordance with applicable Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We do not sell Meta Platform Data or use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Storage, Security, and Retention

Application configuration, logs, scheduled content, media files, and access credentials may be stored where needed to operate the service. The application operator must protect this information, restrict access to authorized people, and delete it when it is no longer needed or when deletion is required.

5. Sharing of Information

Information may be transmitted to Meta or Telegram when necessary to provide the requested features. Information is not sold. Any other sharing must be limited to what is necessary to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

6. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information associated with Auto Post, contact the application operator at the email address below. Please identify the information you want deleted without sending passwords or access tokens. Requests will be reviewed and applicable information will be deleted in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Revoking Access

Users can stop using the application and revoke its Facebook permissions through the relevant Facebook or Meta settings. Revoking permissions may prevent some application features from working. Revoking permissions does not necessarily delete information already stored by the application; deletion requests should also be sent to the contact email below.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
